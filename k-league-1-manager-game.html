What K League 1 is like to manage in

The average player here is 26.9, making it the second oldest squad pool in the game. Expect to be planning succession from your first window. It is also one of the tightest divisions in the game: only 4.9 rating points separate the strongest first eleven from the weakest, so promotion and relegation are decided by management rather than budget. Look for the 47 under-21s who have not finished developing — the squads span 19 nations, so the pool is wider than the league's size suggests.

The board sets a finishing position, and the rest is yours: tactics, selection, the transfer window, contracts, the academy and the cups. Miss the target badly enough and you are sacked. Your save lives in your own browser, with nothing to install and nobody to sign up to.
