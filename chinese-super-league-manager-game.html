What Chinese Super League is like to manage in

The average player here is 27.3, making it the oldest squad pool in the game. Expect to be planning succession from your first window. There are 6.0 rating points between the strongest and weakest first elevens — the favourites are real, but the title is not settled in August. Look for the 87 under-21s who have not finished developing — the squads span 30 nations, so the pool is wider than the league's size suggests.

The board sets a finishing position, and the rest is yours: tactics, selection, the transfer window, contracts, the academy and the cups. Miss the target badly enough and you are sacked. Your save lives in your own browser, with nothing to install and nobody to sign up to.
