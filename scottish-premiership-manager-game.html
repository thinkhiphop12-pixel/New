What Scottish Premiership is like to manage in

The average age across the division is 25.3, which puts it mid-table for experience: a few veterans to move on, a core with years left. It is a top-heavy division — 14.6 rating points separate the best first eleven from the worst — so taking a club from the bottom half to a title is one of the harder saves in the game. 66 players aged 21 or under still have meaningful growth left, drawn from 56 different nations.

You get an objective from the board and a budget that rarely matches it. Everything after that — shape, signings, renewals, youth intake, cup priorities — is your call, and the season simulates on it. No download, no account, save kept in your browser.
