About 7-0-0

7-0-0 is a free online football game where you build the perfect World Cup XI and find out whether it could go an entire tournament unbeaten — 7 games, 7 wins, zero draws, zero losses. Spin for real countries and World Cups, draft your team player by player, and see how your dream line-up would stack up across a full World Cup campaign.

How it works

  1. Pick your mode and formation — choose how you want to play and the shape of your team.
  2. Spin to draw a random country and World Cup year.
  3. Draft one player from that squad into an open position — or take a rare Icon offer.
  4. Repeat until every position is filled.
  5. Get your projected 7-game record and the tier your XI earns — from a Group Stage Exit all the way up to the legendary 7-0-0.

The 7-0-0 challenge

Only one team in World Cup history has ever gone 7-0-0: Brazil's 2002 squad won all seven of their matches — three group games plus the Round of 16, quarter-final, semi-final and final — without a single draw or loss. It's been done exactly once. A flawless run is achievable in the game, but deliberately punishing: you'll need elite players in every position and very little luck to spare.

Formations

Build your XI in any of seven classic shapes: 4-3-3, 4-4-2, 4-2-4, 3-4-3, 3-5-2, 5-3-2 and 5-4-1. Players can only fill positions they genuinely play, so every team you build is realistic.

Game modes

  • Classic — full overall ratings and player stats are on show. Draft the strongest team you can.
  • Expert — ratings and stats are hidden. You see only names and positions and draft on pure football knowledge. The stats and final record are revealed at the end.

Frequently asked questions

Is 7-0-0 free to play?
Yes — it's completely free and runs in your browser. There's nothing to download.
Which countries and tournaments can I draw?
Real World Cup squads from 2002 through to the upcoming 2026 tournament, with squads and player ratings based on the Fjelstul World Cup Database.
Has anyone ever actually gone 7-0-0?
Yes — Brazil's 2002 squad is the only team in World Cup history to win all seven of their matches in a single tournament.
How is my record worked out?
Your projected 7-game record is based on the overall ratings of your XI, weighted by how well each player fits the position you put them in.
What's an Icon offer?
A rare (~11% per spin) chance to draft a legendary player who isn't tied to one squad or tournament, straight into an open position.
Do I need an account?
No sign-up and no app needed — just spin and play.
