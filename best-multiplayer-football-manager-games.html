Best multiplayer Football Manager games

Managing against friends is a whole different experience: here are the best multiplayer options for 2026.

Updated 11 August 2026 · BALLKNW

Short answer

Top Eleven is the best multiplayer football management game for casual players, while a Football Manager 26 online career is the best for serious players who want the full experience against people they know.

Top Eleven

Top Eleven is built around multiplayer. You're managing against other real managers in live leagues. It's social, competitive, and easy to pick up, and it runs on a phone, which is why it has the biggest player base of any manager game.

Football Manager 26 online

FM26 online careers let you manage against friends in the full game. It's the deepest multiplayer experience: all the complexity of FM, but with human beings in the other dugouts, who will absolutely poach your best player.

Other options

  • FM network games: the traditional multiplayer mode over a shared network
  • OFM: free-to-play persistent leagues against a wider pool of managers

House rules worth agreeing first

  • Fixed matchday slot: one night a week, same time, no exceptions
  • Club restrictions: no more than one super-club, or none at all
  • Transfer policy: whether human-to-human deals are allowed, and any minimum fee
  • An exit plan: what happens to a club when someone stops turning up

Common questions

What is the best multiplayer football manager game?

Top Eleven for casual mobile play against friends, and a Football Manager 26 online career when you want real tactical depth.

How do you play Football Manager 26 with friends?

Start an online career, invite the other managers to the save, and everyone takes a club. Matchdays are played when all human managers are online.

Is Top Eleven really multiplayer?

Yes: league fixtures and cup rounds are against squads built by other real managers, though the matches themselves are simulated rather than played live.

Can you play multiplayer Football Manager on mobile?

Not in the full-game sense. For mobile multiplayer, Top Eleven and OFM are the realistic options.

How many managers should an online career have?

Three to six works best. Fewer feels empty, and more makes scheduling matchdays almost impossible.
