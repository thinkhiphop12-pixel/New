🚨 Major Signings & Completed Business

Bruno Guimarães (£75m)

Arsenal officially unveiled Bruno Guimarães following his £75m move from Newcastle United. The Brazilian midfielder made his first appearance in front of supporters at the Emirates Stadium over the weekend.

Summer Additions: Guimarães joins Piero Hincapie, Illan Meslier, and Christos Tzolis as part of Arsenal's key summer additions.

🎯 Key Inbounds & Targets

Ezri Konsa (Aston Villa)

Defensive reinforcement is a top priority with William Saliba and Jurrien Timber facing injury lay-offs. Arsenal are actively pursuing England international Ezri Konsa, though a gap remains between Arsenal's initial £40m proposal and Aston Villa's £60m valuation. Barcelona's Jules Koundé and Como's Jacobo Ramón are being monitored as alternative options.

Kenan Yildiz (Juventus)

With Vinícius Júnior extending his stay at Real Madrid, Arsenal have turned their focus to wide attackers. Talks are underway regarding Juventus forward Kenan Yildiz, while Chelsea's Pedro Neto and PSG's Bradley Barcola remain linked.

📤 Potential Departures & Exits

Gabriel Jesus

Edging closer to an exit, with Serie A side Napoli leading negotiations to sign the Brazilian forward.

Gabriel Martinelli

Subject to an approach from Galatasaray, though Arsenal will only sanction a sale if a replacement winger is secured first.

Reiss Nelson

Advanced talks are taking place over a potential loan move across London to West Ham United.

Martín Zubimendi

Real Madrid have expressed interest in the midfielder, but Arsenal are extremely hesitant to entertain bids after missing out on other central midfield targets.
