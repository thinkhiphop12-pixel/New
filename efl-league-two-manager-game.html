What EFL League Two is like to manage in

The average age across the division is 25.2, which puts it mid-table for experience: a few veterans to move on, a core with years left. A 5.8-point spread between the best and worst first elevens leaves room for a mid-table side to gatecrash the top four. With 169 players aged 21 or under who still have real growth in them, it is one of the better divisions in the game to buy young and sell high.
