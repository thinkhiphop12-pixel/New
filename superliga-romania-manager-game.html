What Superliga is like to manage in

At 26.1 on average these are mature squads without being old ones, so your first window is about quality rather than triage. Strongest to weakest spans 9.1 rating points. Overachieving is possible here; it just costs you a good transfer window. There are 93 under-21s with room to improve here, and 66 nationalities across the division to scout from.

The board sets a finishing position, and the rest is yours: tactics, selection, the transfer window, contracts, the academy and the cups. Miss the target badly enough and you are sacked. Your save lives in your own browser, with nothing to install and nobody to sign up to.
