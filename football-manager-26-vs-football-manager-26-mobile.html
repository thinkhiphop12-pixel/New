Football Manager 26 vs FM26 Mobile

Two games, one name, completely different experiences — here's what you're actually getting with each one.

Updated 11 August 2026 · BALLKNW

Short answer

Football Manager 26 is the full PC game — deep, detailed, and demanding. Football Manager 26 Mobile is a scaled-down version for phones — quicker, simpler, and designed for touchscreens. They share the same name and the same database, but they're different games.

What the full version gives you

The full FM26 is the real deal. You're managing every aspect of the club — tactics, training, transfers, youth development, staff management, media interactions, and more. The match engine is 3D, the database is enormous, and the level of detail is obsessive.

It's the game for people who want to spend hours on a single save, agonising over every decision.

What the mobile version gives you

FM26 Mobile is the same core idea but stripped down. The match engine is simpler, the database is smaller, and the management options are reduced. You can still sign players, pick tactics, and play matches, but it's faster and less detailed.

It's the game for people who want to play on the go, without the time commitment of the full version.

Which one should you buy?

Buy the full version if

You want the complete Football Manager experience, you have time to invest, and you play on PC or Mac.

Buy the mobile version if

You want to play on your phone, you don't have hours to spare, and you want a quicker experience.

Buy neither yet if

You are not sure management games are for you. Play something free in a browser first — Gaffa takes about twenty minutes to find out.

Can you transfer saves between them?

No. They're separate games with separate save files. You can't start a save on mobile and continue it on PC.

Common questions

What is the difference between Football Manager 26 and FM26 Mobile?

The full game is a deep PC and Mac management sim with a 3D match engine and every club department to run. FM26 Mobile is a phone game built on the same database with a simplified match engine and far fewer management options.

Can you transfer an FM26 save to FM26 Mobile?

No. They are separate games with separate save formats, and there is no cross-save between them.

Is FM26 Mobile worth it?

If you want to manage a club in short sessions on a phone, yes. If you want the full simulation with training, staff and media, you will find it thin.

How much does Football Manager 26 cost?

The full game is typically £40–£50 and the mobile version £10–£20, though regional pricing and sales change that regularly.

Does FM26 Mobile have the same database as the full game?

It is drawn from the same research but is smaller — fewer playable leagues and fewer players per club, to keep saves running on a phone.
