Common questions

Are these players free agents?

No, and the difference matters. Every player here is still contracted to a club until 2026. What a short contract changes is the price — a club facing the loss of an asset for nothing is a more willing seller.

Is it better to wait until the contract expires?

Sometimes, but you are betting nobody else moves first and that the player does not simply re-sign. Buying in the last year usually costs a fraction of the same player two seasons earlier.
