FM26: best tactics and formations

The right tactic can turn a mid-table team into title winners: here are the best tactics and formations for FM26.

Updated 11 August 2026 · BALLKNW

Short answer

The best tactics in FM26 depend on your squad, but the most consistently effective formations are the 4-3-3, 4-2-3-1 and 3-5-2. The key is to build a tactic that suits your players, not force your players into a tactic.

Tactical principles that work in FM26

  1. Match your tactic to your players: don't play a high press if your forwards can't press
  2. Balance is key: extreme settings in both directions at once produce chaos, not chances
  3. Adapt to the opposition: the same tactic should not face a title rival and a relegation side
  4. Use player instructions sparingly: tell players what the role doesn't already do, nothing more
  5. Be patient: tactical familiarity takes weeks of training to build, and results before then are noise

Three styles that hold up

The gegenpress

High pressing, quick transitions and intense closing down still overwhelm most AI opponents. It needs a fit, deep squad with high work rate, and it fades badly in fixture pile-ups. See how gegenpressing actually works.

Controlled possession

Keep the ball, control the game, create through patient build-up. Lower risk than pressing, easier on fitness, and it needs technical players more than athletic ones.

Counter-attack

If you're a smaller club, this is your best bet. Sit deep, stay compact, and hit teams on the break with pace. It is the most reliable way to beat a stronger squad.

Building your own instead of downloading one

Downloaded tactics work until they don't: usually the moment your squad stops matching the one they were designed around. Building your own takes an afternoon:

  • Start from your best players and pick a shape that fits them
  • Choose roles that match attributes, not names you like
  • Set a mentality and change as little as possible on top of it
  • Play ten matches before judging it, then change one thing at a time

Common questions

What is the best tactic in Football Manager 26?

There is no single best tactic. A high-pressing 4-3-3 is the most consistently effective at strong clubs, while counter-attacking shapes work better at smaller ones. The best tactic is the one that matches your squad.

Is gegenpressing still good in FM26?

Yes, provided your squad has the work rate and stamina for it and the depth to rotate. It is punishing on fitness during congested periods.

Should I download tactics or make my own?

Make your own. Downloaded tactics assume a specific squad profile, and they stop working the moment your players do not fit it.

How long does a new tactic take to work in FM?

Roughly ten matches, plus the training time needed for tactical familiarity to build. Judging it sooner than that is judging noise.

Why does my team create chances but not score?

Usually chance quality rather than finishing. Too many long shots, not enough players arriving in the box, or a striker whose role pulls them away from goal.
