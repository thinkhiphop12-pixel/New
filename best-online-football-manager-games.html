Best Online Football Manager Games

Managing against real people changes everything — here are the best online football management games for competitive players.

Updated 11 August 2026 · BALLKNW

Short answer

The best online football management games are Top Eleven for live mobile multiplayer, OFM for persistent competitive leagues, and an FM26 online career if you want the full Football Manager experience against friends.

Top Eleven

Top Eleven is the most popular online football manager game. You manage a club against other real managers in live leagues. The social and competitive angle is the main draw — you're playing against real people, not AI.

It is built for phones and short sessions, and it is free to start.

OFM — Online Football Manager

OFM is similar to Top Eleven — free-to-play, competitive leagues against real managers. It's less polished but has its own dedicated player base and a stronger emphasis on league standings that run for months.

Football Manager 26 online

The full FM26 supports online careers, where several human managers share one save. It's the deepest online experience available — the full game, with all its complexity, against real opponents.

It is also the most demanding to organise: everyone has to be online at the same time for matchdays, which is why most online careers die in season two rather than at the final whistle.

Other options

  • FM network games — the traditional multiplayer mode, where several players manage in the same save on a local or hosted network
  • Browser multiplayer — smaller browser games with shared leagues; quality varies enormously

Making an online career survive

Agree a schedule first

Fix a night and a start time before anyone picks a club. Online careers die from calendar problems, not from bad tactics.

Cap the club quality

Everyone taking a super-club makes for a dull league. Mixed reputations create actual competition and a transfer market.

Set transfer rules

Human-to-human transfers are where friendships go to die. Agree in advance whether they are allowed, and at what price.

Common questions

What is the best online football manager game?

Top Eleven for casual live multiplayer on a phone, OFM for persistent competitive leagues, and a Football Manager 26 online career for depth against friends.

Can you play Football Manager 26 with friends?

Yes. The full game supports online careers where several human managers share the same save and play matchdays together.

Are online football manager games free?

Top Eleven and OFM are free to download and monetised through in-app purchases. Football Manager online careers require every participant to own the game.

How many people can play a Football Manager online save?

Multiple human managers can share one save; the practical limit is how many people can reliably show up at the same time each week.

Why do online Football Manager careers fall apart?

Scheduling, almost always. Every human manager has to be present for matchdays, so one unreliable participant slows the whole league down.
