What is a 3-5-2 formation?

The formation that makes full-backs into attackers and centre-backs into playmakers: when it works, it's unstoppable.

Updated 11 August 2026 · BALLKNW

Short answer

The 3-5-2 is a formation with three centre-backs, five midfielders (including two wing-backs), and two strikers. The wing-backs are the key: they provide width, attack, and defend, covering the entire flank.

What it actually looks like

Three centre-backs across the back. Two wing-backs on the flanks: they push up to become wide midfielders in possession and drop back to become full-backs out of possession. Three central midfielders. Two strikers.

The wing-backs are the engine of the system. They have to cover the entire flank, getting forward to provide crosses and tracking back to defend. It's one of the most demanding roles in football.

The three centre-backs give you defensive solidity. With three players in the middle, you can cover the width of the pitch and deal with crosses. The central midfielders control the game.

The wing-back role explained

The wing-back is the defining feature of the 3-5-2. They're not full-backs, and they're not wingers, they're somewhere in between.

Why teams use a 3-5-2

  • Wing-back overloads: two wing-backs give you natural width and attacking threat
  • Defensive solidity: three centre-backs can deal with almost anything
  • Numerical advantage in midfield: three central midfielders against two in a 4-4-2
  • Two strikers: always have a partner up front

The trade-off

The wing-backs have to be exceptional athletes. If they can't get up and down the pitch, the system falls apart. You also need centre-backs who are comfortable on the ball and can step into midfield.

And if a wing-back is caught upfield, the back three has to defend the full width of the pitch on its own, which is how 3-5-2 sides concede.

Famous examples

Antonio Conte's teams (Juventus, Chelsea, Inter Milan) are the most famous exponents. The formation is also popular in Serie A and with national teams that want to be solid defensively.

Common questions

What is a 3-5-2 formation?

Three centre-backs, two wing-backs, three central midfielders and two strikers. The wing-backs supply all of the team's natural width, attacking like wingers and defending like full-backs.

What is the difference between 3-5-2 and 5-3-2?

Nothing structural. They are the same eleven players. A team is described as 3-5-2 when its wing-backs are pushed up in possession and 5-3-2 when they drop in to make a back five while defending.

Why do teams play three at the back?

It gives an extra body centrally against two strikers, lets one centre-back step into midfield with the ball, and frees the full-backs to play as wing-backs high up the pitch.

What is the weakness of a 3-5-2?

The wide areas when a wing-back is caught upfield, and the fitness demand on those two players. It can also struggle for width if the wing-backs are pinned back by opposing wingers.

Which managers are known for the 3-5-2?

Antonio Conte above all, at Juventus, Chelsea, Inter and with Italy. The shape has long been a Serie A staple.
