What Serie A is like to manage in

Average age sits at 25.6. Nobody's squad is falling apart with age, and nobody's is waiting three seasons to arrive. The gap from best squad to worst is 12.1 rating points, which is enough for a pecking order and not enough for a procession. Look for the 104 under-21s who have not finished developing — the squads span 67 nations, so the pool is wider than the league's size suggests.

You get an objective from the board and a budget that rarely matches it. Everything after that — shape, signings, renewals, youth intake, cup priorities — is your call, and the season simulates on it. No download, no account, save kept in your browser.
