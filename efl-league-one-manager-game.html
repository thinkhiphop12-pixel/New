What EFL League One is like to manage in

Average age sits at 24.7. Nobody's squad is falling apart with age, and nobody's is waiting three seasons to arrive. The gap from best squad to worst is 7.0 rating points, which is enough for a pecking order and not enough for a procession. With 176 players aged 21 or under who still have real growth in them, it is one of the better divisions in the game to buy young and sell high.

You get an objective from the board and a budget that rarely matches it. Everything after that — shape, signings, renewals, youth intake, cup priorities — is your call, and the season simulates on it. No download, no account, save kept in your browser.
