FM26: How to Make Money

Running a football club is expensive — here's how to keep the finances healthy and build a sustainable future.

Updated 11 August 2026 · BALLKNW

Short answer

The most effective ways to make money in FM26 are player trading (buy low, develop, sell high), qualifying for European competition, and producing your own youth players. Sustainable clubs are built on financial management, not just results.

Player trading

Player trading is the most reliable way to make money. Buy young players with high potential, develop them, and sell them for a profit. The skill is in identifying talent early and — the harder half — selling at the right time.

The right time is usually one season before you think it is: at peak value, with three years left on the contract and a buyer who has just missed out on someone else.

European competition

Qualifying for the Champions League or Europa League is a transformative financial event. Prize money, broadcast revenue and extra home matches together can double what a mid-sized club earns in a season.

It is also the fastest route to signing better players, because reputation rises with it.

Youth development

Developing your own players is the most cost-effective squad-building there is. An academy graduate costs nothing to acquire, counts as home-grown, and if he is good enough he is pure profit when sold.

The pipeline that produces them is covered in the youth development guide.

The thing that actually bankrupts clubs

It is almost never transfer fees. It is wages.

A transfer fee is a one-off, usually paid in instalments. A wage is a weekly commitment for the length of the contract, and it compounds every time you hand out a raise to keep someone happy. Clubs in trouble in FM are almost always clubs whose wage bill outgrew their income two seasons ago.

Watch the wage bill as a percentage of turnover, not the transfer budget. That single number predicts financial trouble better than anything else on the finances screen.

Financial management tips

  • Keep wages under control — never break your structure for one signing
  • Sell before the decline — a 29-year-old on high wages is a liability in waiting
  • Invest in facilities — training and youth facilities pay back over years, not months
  • Use the board — ask for facility upgrades after good seasons, when they say yes
  • Be patient — financial turnarounds take three or four seasons

Common questions

How do you make money in Football Manager 26?

Player trading is the most controllable route: buy young, develop, sell high. European qualification and youth development are the other two big levers.

What is the biggest cost at a football club in FM?

Wages. Transfer fees are one-off and often staged, but the wage bill is a weekly commitment that grows every time you renegotiate a contract.

Should you expand the stadium in Football Manager?

Only once attendances are consistently near capacity and the club can service the cost. Expanding too early ties up money that would do more in the squad.

When should you sell a player in FM?

At peak value with two or three years left on the contract — before performance declines and before the remaining contract length starts cutting the fee.

How much money does Champions League qualification bring in FM?

Enough to reshape a mid-sized club's finances in a single season, through prize money, broadcast revenue and extra home matches. It is the single largest jump in income available.
