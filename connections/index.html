Connections XI

Find four groups of four. Watch out for the traps.

What is Connections XI?

Connections XI is a daily football grouping puzzle. You're shown sixteen footballing names — players, clubs, countries, stadiums or terms — and your job is to sort them into the four hidden groups of four. Each group shares a theme, but some names look like they fit more than one category. That's the trap.

How to play

  • Tap four tiles you think belong together, then press Submit.
  • Solve a group and it locks in, colour-coded by difficulty: yellow easiest, purple trickiest.
  • You can make four mistakes before the game ends. A new puzzle arrives every day.
