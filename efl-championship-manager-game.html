What EFL Championship is like to manage in

Squads here average 25.1 years old — settled enough to compete now, young enough that you are not forced into an immediate rebuild. It is also one of the tightest divisions in the game: only 5.5 rating points separate the strongest first eleven from the weakest, so promotion and relegation are decided by management rather than budget. Look for the 157 under-21s who have not finished developing — the squads span 67 nations, so the pool is wider than the league's size suggests.

You get an objective from the board and a budget that rarely matches it. Everything after that — shape, signings, renewals, youth intake, cup priorities — is your call, and the season simulates on it. No download, no account, save kept in your browser.
