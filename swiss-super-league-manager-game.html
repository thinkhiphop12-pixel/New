What Swiss Super League is like to manage in

Squads here average 24.0 years old — settled enough to compete now, young enough that you are not forced into an immediate rebuild. There are 7.0 rating points between the strongest and weakest first elevens — the favourites are real, but the title is not settled in August. Look for the 105 under-21s who have not finished developing — the squads span 56 nations, so the pool is wider than the league's size suggests.

The board sets a finishing position, and the rest is yours: tactics, selection, the transfer window, contracts, the academy and the cups. Miss the target badly enough and you are sacked. Your save lives in your own browser, with nothing to install and nobody to sign up to.
