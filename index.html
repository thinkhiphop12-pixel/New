About Ball Knowledge

Ball Knowledge is a small hub of free, browser-based football knowledge games. No accounts, no downloads — pick a game and start playing straight away.

Our games

  • 7-0-0 — spin for real World Cup squads from 2002 onward, draft a player into your XI each round, and find out whether your dream team could go a whole tournament unbeaten: 7 games, 7 wins, zero draws, zero losses.
  • Dynasty — draft your squad with bench options, manage injuries and rotations, and navigate the World Cup tournament with live match play. Combine squad building with tournament action.
  • Lineup — given a real match or tournament, place every starting player in their correct position from memory before the clock runs out.
  • Bracket — simulate the FIFA World Cup 2026. A real Dixon-Coles statistical match engine runs the full 48-team tournament, group stage through final, or thousands of Monte Carlo runs for each team's odds of winning it all.
  • Boardroom — a retro football club management game. Sign players, set your lineup, train your squad, manage the stadium and sponsors, and build a dynasty across multiple seasons of promotion and relegation.
  • Scout — a daily World Cup player guessing game. One mystery footballer, six guesses, with clues on nation, position, era, rating and pace after every try.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ball Knowledge free to play?
Yes — every game here is completely free and runs in your browser. There's nothing to download.
Do I need to make an account?
No. No sign-up, no app — just open a game and play.
Where does the player and squad data come from?
Real World Cup squads and results, based on public football datasets including the Fjelstul World Cup Database. Ratings used in-game are stylized approximations for gameplay, not official figures.
Is this an official FIFA or football federation product?
No — Ball Knowledge is an independent, unofficial fan project, not affiliated with FIFA, the Premier League, or any club or federation.
