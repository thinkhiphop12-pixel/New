About Ball Knowledge

Ball Knowledge is a small, independent hub of free football games and puzzles. There are no accounts and nothing to download — open a game and play. Most of our puzzles refresh once a day, so there's always a reason to come back tomorrow.

Daily puzzles

  • Footle — a football take on the daily five-letter word game. Everyone gets the same word each day; share your grid without spoiling the answer.
  • Connections XI — sixteen names, four hidden categories. Work out which players, clubs or countries belong together.
  • Pitch Strands — a themed word-search where every answer ties to the day's football theme, plus a hidden spangram that spans the board.
  • Starting XI — spell football words around the sides of a square, using letters from different sides in turn.

Bigger games

  • 7-0-0 — search real World Cup squads from 1930 to 2026, draft a starting XI, and simulate the seven games it takes to win the tournament. Go 7 wins, 0 draws, 0 losses for a perfect run.
  • Dynasty — squad building with a bench, injuries and rotation across a full tournament.
  • Lineup — place every starter from a famous match in their correct position before the clock runs out.
  • Bracket — a Dixon-Coles statistical engine simulates the 2026 World Cup, single run or thousands of Monte-Carlo runs for each nation's title odds.
  • Boardroom — a retro club-management campaign across multiple seasons.
  • Retro Manager — a nostalgic football management sim: choose a club, run an 18-team league, and handle transfers, training, tactics, stadium and sponsors season after season.

World Cup squads & ratings

Browse full World Cup squads and player ratings for every major nation from 1930 to 2026 — positions, overall ratings and the strongest XI for each team, from Brazil 2002 to Argentina 2022.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ball Knowledge free to play?
Yes — every game here is completely free and runs in your browser. There's nothing to download.
Do I need an account?
No. No sign-up, no app — just open a game and play. Streaks are saved on your device.
How often do the puzzles change?
Footle, Connections XI, Pitch Strands and Starting XI all refresh once a day at midnight, and everyone gets the same puzzle.
Where does the player and squad data come from?
Real World Cup squads and results, based on public football datasets including historical FIFA records and the Fjelstul World Cup Database. In-game ratings are stylised approximations for gameplay, not official figures.
Is this an official FIFA product?
No — Ball Knowledge is an independent, unofficial fan project, not affiliated with FIFA, the Premier League, or any club or federation.
