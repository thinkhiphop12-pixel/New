What Does a Football Manager Do?

The job behind the touchline arm-waving, explained properly.

The short answer

A football manager picks the team, sets the tactics, runs the squad, and answers for the results. On any given week that means choosing a starting XI and a shape, preparing the players in training, deciding who to buy, sell and re-sign, keeping thirty professional egos pointed in the same direction, and standing in front of the press whether the weekend went well or not. It's part tactician, part talent scout, part psychologist, part public spokesperson — and the results column judges all of it.

The five real responsibilities

1. Selection and tactics

Choosing the eleven, the formation and the game plan for each opponent — and changing it mid-match when it isn't working. Roles like the false 9 or a low block are the manager's tools here.

2. Training and development

Setting the weekly training programme, improving individual players, and bringing academy prospects through to the first team.

3. Transfers and contracts

Identifying targets, negotiating fees and wages, and trimming the squad — in the British "manager" model at least. Many European clubs split this off to a sporting director and call the coach a head coach instead.

4. Man-management

Dressing-room morale, dropped players, contract sulks, captaincy calls. Most managers who fail don't fail on tactics — they lose the dressing room.

5. Media and the board

Press conferences, fan expectations, and above them a board with its own goals. Managers are hired knowing that results, not effort, decide how long they keep the job.

Manager vs head coach vs gaffer

"Manager" in the full British sense means control of both coaching and squad-building. A head coach handles training and matchday while recruitment belongs to a sporting director — the standard structure across most of Europe. And "gaffer" is simply what the players call whoever's in charge; we've covered the word's origin in what is a gaffer in football? and there's a full football terms glossary if you want more.

Quick answers

What does a football manager do on matchday?

Final team selection, the pre-match talk, in-game tactical changes and substitutions, then post-match media. The week's work is done before kickoff — matchday is about reacting well.

Do football managers pick the team or does the club?

The manager or head coach picks the starting XI — that's the one duty that never gets delegated. Which players are at the club to pick from is where boards and sporting directors get involved.

Is being a football manager a full-time job?

Completely — and famously insecure. The average tenure of a manager in England's professional leagues is under two years, and sackings mid-season are routine.
