What Is a Number 10?

The shirt number that became a position — the player who makes things happen, the one fans love, the one who gets the ball and makes magic.

Updated 11 August 2026 · BALLKNW

Short answer

A number 10 is an attacking midfielder who operates in the space behind the striker(s), creating chances, scoring goals, and dictating the attack. It's the most glamorous role in football — the playmaker, the creator, the one who makes the team tick.

What it actually looks like

The number 10 sits between the opposition's midfield and defensive lines — the pocket. He receives the ball with his back to goal, turns, and looks for a pass. He plays through-balls, he shoots from distance, he drifts into wide areas to create overloads.

He doesn't do much defending. His job is to save his energy for the moments that matter — the pass that splits the defence, the shot that wins the game, the moment of brilliance that changes everything.

The number 10 is the player everyone wants to be. The one with the flair, the vision, the technique. The one who makes the game look beautiful.

The three things a number 10 does

  1. Creates chances — through-balls, crosses, clever passes that unlock defences
  2. Scores goals — arriving late in the box, shooting from distance, free-kicks
  3. Links play — connecting the midfield to the attack

Why teams use a number 10

  • To unlock deep defences — the number 10 finds the gaps between the lines
  • To create chances — he's the team's primary creator
  • Because you have a special player — not every squad has one, but if you do, you use him

The trade-off

The number 10 doesn't defend much. You're essentially playing with ten men out of possession. Modern football has moved away from the traditional number 10, preferring more industrious midfielders, but the role still exists in various forms.

Famous examples

Diego Maradona, Zinedine Zidane, Ronaldinho, Mesut Özil, and more recently players like Kevin De Bruyne (though he's more of a hybrid) or Bruno Fernandes.

Common questions

What is a number 10 in football?

An attacking midfielder who plays in the space behind the striker, acting as the team's main creator. The name comes from the shirt number traditionally worn by the player in that position.

What is the difference between a number 10 and a false 9?

A number 10 starts in the pocket behind the striker. A false 9 starts as the striker and drops back into that same pocket, dragging a centre-back out of position on the way.

Is the number 10 dying out?

The pure version is rarer, because pressing systems want attacking midfielders who defend. The creative job still exists, but it is more often given to a wide forward or an advanced central midfielder.

What formation uses a number 10?

Most commonly a 4-2-3-1, where the 10 sits between two holding midfielders and the lone striker, or a 4-4-2 diamond, where he plays at the tip.

Who is the greatest number 10 of all time?

Diego Maradona is the usual answer, with Zinedine Zidane, Michel Platini and Ronaldinho in the same conversation.
