Is Football Manager 26 worth buying?

The annual question: is this year's version worth your money, or should you stick with last year's game?

Updated 11 August 2026 · BALLKNW

Short answer

Football Manager 26 is worth buying if you're a dedicated FM player who wants the latest database, updated features and the most polished version. If you're happy with FM24 or FM25 and don't care about squad accuracy, you can comfortably skip it.

What changes in a new FM

Each year's Football Manager brings incremental improvements rather than revolutionary changes. The pattern is consistent:

  • Updated database: new signings, new ratings, new wonderkids, corrected squads
  • Match engine work: better AI decision-making and more realistic movement
  • New features: usually one or two headline additions rather than an overhaul
  • Interface changes: refinements, occasionally a larger redesign

The database is the part that genuinely expires. Everything else is a matter of degree.

The value proposition

FM26 typically costs £40-£50. You're paying for the latest database, the updated match engine, and ongoing patches through the season. If you play regularly, the cost per hour is among the lowest in gaming. If you play one season a year, last year's game does the same job.

Prices vary by region and storefront, and the game is routinely discounted a few months after release.

Reasons to wait

Launch patches

Every FM has an early patch cycle. Waiting a month usually means a more stable match engine and a corrected database.

The winter update

The January transfer update refreshes squads mid-season. If you only run one save a year, starting after it is the better-informed choice.

You already have a save you love

A ten-season career on an older version is worth more than a fresh start on a new one. Finish it.

Try before you buy

Sports Interactive publish a demo most years, which is the only honest way to judge whether the match engine feels right to you. If you have never played a management game at all, spend twenty minutes on a free browser manager like Gaffa first: it answers the "do I actually enjoy this?" question for nothing.

Common questions

Is Football Manager 26 worth buying?

If you play regularly or are coming from FM24 or older, yes. If you own FM25 and do not need current squads, the upgrade is optional.

How much does Football Manager 26 cost?

Typically £40-£50 for the full PC and Mac version, with Touch and Mobile versions cheaper. Regional pricing and sales vary.

What is new in Football Manager 26?

The usual annual pattern: an updated database, match-engine and AI improvements, interface refinements and one or two headline features. Check the official release notes for the specifics.

Should I buy FM26 or stick with FM25?

Stick with FM25 if you are mid-save and happy. Upgrade if you want accurate current squads or have been affected by issues fixed since.

Is there a Football Manager 26 demo?

Sports Interactive have published a demo for most recent releases, letting you play a limited portion before buying. Check the store page for availability.
