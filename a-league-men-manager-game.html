What A-League Men is like to manage in

Average age sits at 24.8. Nobody's squad is falling apart with age, and nobody's is waiting three seasons to arrive. It is also one of the tightest divisions in the game: only 4.1 rating points separate the strongest first eleven from the weakest, so promotion and relegation are decided by management rather than budget. Look for the 105 under-21s who have not finished developing — the squads span 27 nations, so the pool is wider than the league's size suggests.

You get an objective from the board and a budget that rarely matches it. Everything after that — shape, signings, renewals, youth intake, cup priorities — is your call, and the season simulates on it. No download, no account, save kept in your browser.
