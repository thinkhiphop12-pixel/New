Best single-player Football Manager games

Some people prefer managing alone: here are the best single-player football management games for 2026.

Updated 11 August 2026 · BALLKNW

Short answer

Football Manager 26 is the best single-player football management game by a huge margin. Nothing else comes close for depth, detail or longevity, but lighter alternatives suit players with less time.

Football Manager 26 (full)

FM26 is the ultimate single-player football management experience. You can spend hundreds of hours on a single save, building a club from the bottom to the top. The AI is challenging, the database is enormous, and the level of detail is unmatched.

It is also the only one of these games where a save can genuinely last years of real time.

Football Manager 26 Touch

Touch is the streamlined version: less admin, more focus on tactics and transfers. Still single-player, but quicker and less demanding, and a season fits comfortably into a few evenings.

Other options

  • FM26 Mobile: single-player and portable, but much simpler
  • Gaffa: browser-based, single-player, no download and no account (our own game)
  • Older FM versions: FM24 and FM25 are still excellent and much cheaper

Why single-player still wins

Playing alone means the save waits for you. You can spend forty minutes on one transfer negotiation, restart a season, or leave the game untouched for a month and pick it up exactly where it was.

It also means the difficulty is yours to set. Take a bottom-division club with no budget and the game is as hard as any online league: without needing anyone else to turn up.

Common questions

What is the best single-player football manager game?

Football Manager 26 on PC or Mac. Nothing else offers the same database size, AI quality or save longevity.

Can you play Football Manager offline?

Largely yes: once activated, single-player careers do not need a constant connection, though the storefront client may need to check in periodically.

What is a good single-player alternative to Football Manager?

FM26 Touch for a lighter version of the same thing, and browser managers like Gaffa when you want a season in an evening with nothing to install.

How long does a Football Manager save last?

As long as you want. Many players run saves across twenty or more in-game seasons and hundreds of real hours.

Is Football Manager hard for beginners?

It is deep rather than difficult. Starting with a well-resourced club in a familiar league, letting your assistant handle training and keeping tactics simple makes the first season very manageable.
