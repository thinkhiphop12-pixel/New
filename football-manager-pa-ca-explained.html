PA & CA in Football Manager, explained

Maximum PA, negative PA ranges, and how player potential actually works.

The short answer

Football Manager rates every player on two hidden 1-200 scales. Current Ability (CA) is how good they are today; Potential Ability (PA) is their ceiling. The maximum PA is 200, reserved for generational talents: in practice anything from 180 up is world-class potential, and a solid top-flight starter sits around 140-160. Neither number is shown in-game; scouts and coaches report star ratings that estimate them.

The PA scale at a glance

PA rangeWhat it means
190-200Generational: Ballon d'Or ceiling
170-189World-class potential
150-169Top-division quality, possible international
130-149Solid top-flight / strong second-tier player
100-129Lower-league professional
Below 100Semi-pro and grassroots levels

Negative PA: why -10 doesn't mean bad

Young players in the database often have negative PA, which confuses everyone the first time. A negative value is a random range rolled fresh in every save:

-10 → rolls 170-200

The elite wonderkid band. In one save he's a 172, in another a 198.

-9.5 → rolls 160-190

Very high potential, just below the top band.

-9 → rolls 150-180, and so on

Each half-step drops the range by ten points. It's why wonderkid lists differ between saves, and why scouting in your save beats any online list.

Whether a player ever reaches his rolled PA depends on training, minutes, injuries and hidden personality attributes like professionalism and ambition. Plenty of 180-PA teenagers retire as 140-CA journeymen.

Other abbreviations that trip people up

Int in squad views is international caps (or international reputation in scouting contexts), not a coached attribute. The visible attributes are the 1-20 ratings like Passing, Vision and Work Rate. Quick answers

What is the maximum PA in Football Manager?

200. The scale runs 1-200 and only generational players get close to the top. Anything above 180 is already world-class potential.

Can a player's PA change during a save?

Ordinarily no: PA is fixed once the save rolls it. CA moves constantly within that ceiling based on development and decline.

How do I see a player's PA in-game?

You can't see the raw number without an editor: scouts and coaches give star-rating estimates instead, and their judging attributes decide how accurate those stars are.
