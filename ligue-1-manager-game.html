What Ligue 1 is like to manage in

The average age across the division is 24.3, which puts it mid-table for experience: a few veterans to move on, a core with years left. It is a top-heavy division — 14.8 rating points separate the best first eleven from the worst — so taking a club from the bottom half to a title is one of the harder saves in the game. Look for the 163 under-21s who have not finished developing — the squads span 69 nations, so the pool is wider than the league's size suggests.

You get an objective from the board and a budget that rarely matches it. Everything after that — shape, signings, renewals, youth intake, cup priorities — is your call, and the season simulates on it. No download, no account, save kept in your browser.
