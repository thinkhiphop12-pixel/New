About BALLKNW

BALLKNW is a free browser-based football management game designed for football knowledge enthusiasts who want to test and grow their understanding of the sport — quickly, daily, and with friends.

Our Mission

We believe football knowledge games should be:

  • Free. No paywall, no premium tiers, no "energy" mechanics.
  • Fast. Play a full game in 2–5 minutes on any device.
  • Built on real data. Every squad, every player rating, every match — sourced from public, verifiable football datasets.
  • Privacy-respecting. No sign-up, no accounts, no tracking beyond consent-based advertising.
  • Shareable. Every result converts to an emoji grid you can paste into Slack, Twitter, Discord, or group chats.

The Game

Gaffa — Manage a club across 28 leagues in 25 countries — 494 clubs and nearly 14,000 players, with current-season squads. Build tactics, handle transfers, contracts, cups, and youth development across a full season. Your save lives in your browser.

Why We Built This

We're football obsessives who grew up testing our knowledge against friends via daily trivia, fantasy leagues, and manager simulations. We realized the best football games combine quick play sessions (5 mins), real data, and genuine engagement without exploitative mechanics.

Most football games today are either casual tap-games or deep time-sinks. We wanted to fill the gap: games that respect your time, test your knowledge, and stay free forever.

Data & Transparency

All player squads and World Cup data come from public sources (current-season club rosters, official FIFA records, historical Wikipedia archives). In-game player ratings are our own stylized approximations designed for gameplay balance, not official figures from any third-party ratings provider.

Who Builds This

BALLKNW is an independent, fan-made project built for football tactics enthusiasts. Our simulation engine, Gaffa, uses publicly available football datasets to construct realistic seasonal arrays, ratings, and match calculations. It is built and maintained by a small independent team rather than a studio, and it is funded entirely by light, consent-based advertising — there is no paid tier, no premium currency, and no investor pressure to add one.

Everything on the site is written by people who actually play the genre. Our tactical explainers describe systems we have set up and tested inside our own engine, which is why they focus on what a shape costs you over a season rather than on abstract theory.
