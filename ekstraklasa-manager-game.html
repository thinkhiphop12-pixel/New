What Ekstraklasa is like to manage in

Average age sits at 25.3. Nobody's squad is falling apart with age, and nobody's is waiting three seasons to arrive. The gap from best squad to worst is 6.7 rating points, which is enough for a pecking order and not enough for a procession. Look for the 106 under-21s who have not finished developing — the squads span 65 nations, so the pool is wider than the league's size suggests.

The board sets a finishing position, and the rest is yours: tactics, selection, the transfer window, contracts, the academy and the cups. Miss the target badly enough and you are sacked. Your save lives in your own browser, with nothing to install and nobody to sign up to.
