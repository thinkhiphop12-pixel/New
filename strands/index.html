What is Pitch Strands?

Pitch Strands is a daily football word-search. Every puzzle has a theme — shown at the top — and the board hides a handful of words that all fit it, plus one special spangram: a longer word or phrase, tied to the theme, that touches two sides of the board. Find them all to finish the day's puzzle.

How to play

  • Tap a letter, then tap connected letters (including diagonals) to trace a word.
  • Spell a theme word and it locks in green; the spangram locks in blue.
  • Find every theme word to complete the board. A new theme every day.
