What is Starting XI?

Starting XI is a daily football letter puzzle. Twelve letters sit around a square, three to a side. Spell football words using those letters — but consecutive letters must come from different sides of the square. Each new word has to start with the last letter of your previous word. The aim: use all twelve letters in as few words as possible.

How to play

  • Tap letters to build a word; you can't use two letters from the same side in a row.
  • Press Enter to lock a word in. Your next word must begin with that word's last letter.
  • Cover all twelve letters to finish. There's always at least one two-word football solution.
