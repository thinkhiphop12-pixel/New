Footle

Guess the hidden 5-letter football word in six tries.

What is Footle?

Footle is a daily football word game — a football-flavoured take on the classic five-letter guessing puzzle. Every day there's one hidden football word: a piece of footballing vocabulary or a famous surname. You get six guesses. After each guess, tiles turn green when a letter is in the right place, yellow when the letter is in the word but in the wrong spot, and grey when it isn't in the word at all.

How to play Footle

  • Type any five-letter word and press Enter to guess.
  • Use the colour clues to narrow down the hidden football word.
  • Solve it in six tries to keep your streak alive — a fresh Footle drops every day at midnight.
