The urgent one

Act on this first

Every single one of the 35 referring domains is spam, and all of them appeared between 28 June and today. There is no legitimate link mixed in.

The profile is made up of Fiverr-gig link farms (seopxl-ranking-boost-lab.shop, fiverr-cost-effective-seo.site and nine more of that pattern), casino and gambling spam, expired-domain PBNs, and scraped directories. Anchor text is 51 text links into a football site from laundry, crypto and "celebrity news" domains.

If someone bought an SEO gig recently, this is what was delivered. If not, it's a scraper network or negative SEO. Either way the +126.67% referring domains figure — the healthiest-looking number on the dashboard — is the one actively suppressing the site.

I've generated a ready-to-upload disavow file covering all 35, grouped and annotated by category: disavow-ballknw.txt. Review it, then upload at Search Console's disavow tool. It's reversible, but give it weeks to take effect. Note that the disavow tool does not accept Domain properties — it needs a URL-prefix property.

Why 18 pages rank for nothing

I audited the site expecting to find broken technical SEO. I didn't. Canonicals are consistent and correct across all 19 pages, the sitemap is complete, robots.txt is permissive, internal linking is genuinely good (every article is linked from the homepage and from 3–9 sibling pages), and structured data is thorough — Article, FAQPage and DefinedTerm schema are already in place on the content pages.

The plan

Ordered by leverage. The first phase is genuinely tonight's work; the rest is calendar time you can't compress.

PHASE 01 Tonight ~2 hours

Stop the bleeding, start the clock

  • Upload the disavow file. disavow-ballknw.txt is written and ready — review it, then submit in Search Console. Nothing else you do matters as much.
  • Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console, then use URL Inspection → Request Indexing on all 18 unranked pages individually. This is the single biggest accelerator available; it turns months of passive discovery into days.
  • Do the same in Bing Webmaster Tools — BingSiteAuth.xml is already in the repo, and Bing's IndexNow submits instantly.
  • Add US-English phrasing to the false 9, low block and gaffer pages: an FAQ entry each answering "what is a false 9 in soccer", "what is a low block in soccer", "what is a gaffer in soccer", with the soccer wording in the <h2> and in the existing FAQPage schema. That's ~2,600/mo of addressable volume the pages currently spell wrong.

Realistic by morning: nothing visible. Indexing takes days. This phase sets everything else in motion.

PHASE 02 Days 2–14 passive

Let the queue drain

  • Pages get crawled and enter the index. Expect the keyword count to move in steps, not a curve — a page lands and brings 3–6 keywords at once.
  • Watch Search Console's Pages report for anything marked Discovered – currently not indexed. That's the one status that signals a real problem rather than a wait.
  • Don't touch published content in this window. Editing resets crawl scheduling on pages that are mid-assessment.

Expected: 15–30 organic keywords, most on page 3–5. Traffic still ~0.

PHASE 03 Weeks 3–10 the 50

Cross 50 keywords

This is where the target is actually hit, and it needs no new pages — the four clusters below already have published articles behind them.

  • Deepen rather than widen. Each existing page should cover its cluster's variants explicitly — headings and FAQ entries for "false 9 formation", "433 false 9", "false 9 meaning" on the one page, rather than new thin pages for each.
  • Fix the four page-5 rankings first. gaffer soccer, gaffer football and the two others sit at #52–57 on a page that already ranks #9 for a sibling term. Moving those to page 2 is the cheapest traffic on the site.
  • Then, and only then, add new content — the FM26 cluster (fm 26 database, fm26 mods, football manager 2026 system requirements) is KD 7–16 and seasonally hot right now.

Expected: 50–65 organic keywords — target met — and first real traffic.

PHASE 04 Months 3–12 the slow part

Authority, honestly

  • Real links only: football subreddits and forums where the tactical explainers genuinely answer a question, FM community sites, guest pieces on established football-writing outlets. Ten real links beat a thousand of what's there now.
  • Re-audit the backlink profile monthly and disavow new spam as it appears — the current campaign is ongoing, not finished.
  • AI Visibility follows organic with a lag. Nothing extra to do; llms.txt and the schema already handle it.

Expected at 12 months: Authority Score 8–15, 150–400 keywords. Not 50 AS — that isn't a one-year number for any new domain.
