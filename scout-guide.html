Scout Game Guide

What is Scout?

Scout is a daily football connections puzzle where you group 16 players into 4 themed categories. You have 4 wrong guesses to solve it and build your streak. New puzzle every day at midnight UTC.

How to Play

  1. You'll see 16 footballer names on screen.
  2. Identify connections between players — usually 4+ players share a hidden theme.
  3. Tap or click 4 players you think share a theme, then submit.
  4. If you're wrong, you lose 1 guess (max 4 wrong guesses allowed).
  5. Solve the puzzle to extend your streak and share your emoji grid result.

Theme Types

Scout rotates four types of hidden themes:

🌍 Shared Nation

All 4 players have played international football for the same country. Example: "All played for Brazil" — includes current and historical players.

⚽ Position

All 4 players share the same position on the field. Examples: Strikers, Midfielders, Defenders, Goalkeepers. Position groups rotate daily.

📊 Rating Tier

All 4 players fall into the same in-game rating bracket. Examples: Rated 90+ (icon class), 85–89 (world class), 80–84 (elite), 74–79 (very good).

🏆 World Cup Year

All 4 players featured in the same World Cup. Example: "All featured at the 2022 World Cup" includes squad members from any team.

Winning Strategies

1. Start with Confident Groups

Identify the group you're most confident about first. If you spot 4 players from the same nation or position clearly, submit that immediately. Getting one right removes confusion and gives you a confidence boost.

2. Watch for Overlaps

Players can overlap — a midfielder might be rated 90+ AND played for Brazil. If you're unsure which group a player belongs to, note the overlap and use it to deduce themes.

3. Look for Rare Names

Recognize players from specific eras or competitions. For example, if you see players you associate with a specific World Cup (2022, 2018, 2010), that's a strong signal for a World Cup year theme.

4. Use the Shuffle

Stuck on a group? Use the Shuffle button to reorder the board. Sometimes a fresh arrangement sparks recognition of a theme you missed.

5. Build Your Streak Slowly

It's better to use 3 guesses to solve one puzzle than to rush and waste all 4. Each correct puzzle extends your streak by 1 day. Miss a day, and your streak resets.

Daily Streak Tips

  • Return every day: You need to solve the puzzle before midnight UTC to extend your streak. Missing one day resets it to 0.
  • Play early: Solve Scout in the morning to ensure you don't forget before the reset.
  • Mistakes are learning: Each wrong guess reveals an incorrect connection, ruling it out for future puzzles.
  • Share your result: After solving, you can share a spoiler-free emoji grid showing your solve path.

Advanced Tips

  • Scout uses real player data: Current-season club rosters and every World Cup from 1930–2026. If you know real football, you have an advantage.
  • Ratings are stylized for gameplay, not official figures. A player rated 88 in Scout may have a different official rating; focus on the tier, not the exact number.
  • Position data includes primary position only. A player who can play both midfield and defense will appear as one position.
  • If a puzzle feels hard, note the player names you recognize and the eras they're from. That's often the key to World Cup year themes.
