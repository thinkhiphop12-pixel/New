Common questions

How is a bargain defined here?

A player valued at £2m or less whose potential is at least eight rating points above their current ability. Both conditions matter: cheap players who cannot improve are not bargains, and neither are good players who cost a fortune.

Why are so many of them in smaller leagues?

Because value tracks reputation as well as ability. A player at a lower-ranked club is priced by what their league is worth, which is exactly the gap you are trying to exploit.

Do these prices change during a save?

Yes. Value moves with form, age and contract length, so a bargain spotted early gets more expensive as they develop. The listed price is the starting point of a new game.
