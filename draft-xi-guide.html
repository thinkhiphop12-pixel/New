What is Draft XI?

Spin a real squad pool, draft exactly 11 players into your formation, then simulate a full season chasing Champions, Invincibles or The Treble.

Getting Started

01

Choose your squad pool

Current Premier League, any club squad, or a World Cup year (1930–2026).

02

Select your formation

4-4-2 balanced, 4-3-3 attacking, 3-5-2 wing-heavy, 5-3-2 defensive.

Drafting Strategy

Balance your lineup

One elite keeper, 3–4 strong defenders and midfielders, 2–3 strikers.

Position specialization

A natural fit beats a higher rating out of position.

Formation fit

Draft players who suit the shape you picked, not the other way round.

Depth over stars

Eleven solid 82–85s beat three 90s propping up a weak rest of the XI.

Season Simulation & Objectives

Simulate a full 38-round season against opponents from your pool. Stronger XIs beat harder fields.

Champions: finish 1st.
Invincibles: go unbeaten.
The Treble: league + both cups.
Top 4: finish in the top four.
Escape relegation: avoid the bottom three.

Advanced Tactics

Exploit weak squads

Shape your XI around whatever position your pool is thin on.

Historical eras matter

Older World Cups have thinner pools — expectations shift by year.

Formation adaptation

Short on elite strikers? Switch formation rather than force it.
