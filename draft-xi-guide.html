Draft XI Squad Building Guide

What is Draft XI?

Draft XI is a squad-building and season simulation game. You spin a pool of real footballers (from the current Premier League, any club squad, or a historical World Cup), draft exactly 11 players into your perfect formation, then simulate a full season. Chase seasonal objectives like Champions (league title), Invincibles (unbeaten), or The Treble (league + two cups).

Getting Started

1. Choose Your Squad Pool

Premier League (Current): All 20 PL clubs, 11 starting players per team. Balanced, familiar squads with high-rated players.

Club Squad: Spin a specific Premier League, Championship, or international club's full squad. Smaller pools mean more repeats; great for testing weak squads.

World Cup (1930–2026): Pick any World Cup year and draft from all competing nations. Mix historical legends with modern stars. 1950 Brazil plays 2022 France.

2. Select Your Formation

Choose a formation that shapes your tactical approach:

  • 4-4-2: Classic, balanced. 4 defenders, 4 midfielders, 2 strikers. Solid defense, direct attack.
  • 4-3-3: Attacking midfield focus. 4 defenders, 3 midfielders, 3 forwards. Faster buildup, press higher.
  • 3-5-2: Wing-heavy. 3 defenders, 5 midfielders, 2 strikers. Requires strong wingers, vulnerable to counterattacks.
  • 5-3-2: Defensive. 5 defenders, 3 midfielders, 2 strikers. Safe, absorbs pressure, lower scoring.

Drafting Strategy

Balance Your Lineup

Don't draft 3 world-class strikers and weak defenders. A 90-rated striker is wasted if your defense is 75-rated and concedes 5 goals per game. Aim for:

  • 1 elite goalkeeper (85+)
  • 3–4 quality defenders (80+)
  • 3–4 dynamic midfielders (80+, mix of creative and defensive)
  • 2–3 strikers (80+, at least one 85+)

Position Specialization

Players perform better in their natural positions. A winger forced to play center mid loses chemistry. When drafting, prioritize natural position fits over overall rating. An 82-rated natural left back beats a 86-rated center mid playing left back.

Formation Fit

If you picked 4-3-3 (3 forwards), draft 3 natural forwards or attacking midfielders. If you picked 3-5-2 (5 midfielders), load your midfield with playmakers and ball carriers. Don't pick a formation then draft players that don't fit it.

Depth Over Stars

A lineup of 11 players rated 82–85 will outsimulate a lineup of 3 rated 90+ and 8 rated 75. Consistency wins. Aim for balanced quality across all 11 starting positions.

Season Simulation & Objectives

After drafting, you simulate a full season (38 rounds) playing against opponents from your squad pool. The simulation uses your XI's strengths to generate match results. The harder the opponent, the better your team needs to be.

Seasonal Objectives

Champions: Finish 1st in the league. Win the most points over 38 rounds. Requires a balanced, competitive XI.
Invincibles: Go unbeaten all season (no losses). You can draw, but every match must not be a loss. Harder than Champions — requires elite defense and composure.
The Treble: Win the league + both cup competitions. Requires the strongest XI; only possible if your team is world-class across all positions.
Top 4: Finish in the top 4 finishers. Easier than Champions; a mid-tier XI can achieve this.
Escape Relegation: Finish in the top 20 (avoid bottom 3). Challenge mode with a weak XI.

Understanding Simulation Results

Season results depend on your XI's combined quality. A lineup averaging 82-rated will usually finish mid-table. A lineup averaging 86+ will challenge for Champions. World Cup 1950 with 90-rated players from any country will win; World Cup 2010 with average-rated players will struggle. The simulation is balanced but not fixed — even strong teams can have unlucky seasons.

Advanced Tactics

Exploit Weak Squads

If you spin a squad with one weak position (poor defense, thin midfield), draft a XI that avoids exposing that weakness. If defenders are weak, load your midfield with defensive anchors. If midfield is weak, rely on direct attacks with strong strikers.

Historical Eras Matter

World Cup 1950 has fewer, lower-rated players overall. World Cup 2022 has deep talent. A World Cup 1950 draft needs elite players to dominate; a 2022 draft can succeed with mid-tier players because everyone is stronger. Adjust your expectations per era.

Formation Adaptation

If you're struggling to find 3 elite strikers, don't force 4-3-3 with 3 forwards. Pick 4-4-2 instead and use strong midfielders. Flexibility in formation choice beats stubbornness.
