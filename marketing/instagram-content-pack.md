**Tips:**
- Reels > static posts for reach; even a 10-second screen recording of the
  emoji grid filling in performs well.
- Always reply to the first 5 comments within the hour — it boosts distribution.
- Post between 17:00–21:00 local time (football audience scrolls after work/school).
- The shareable emoji grid is your growth engine: end every caption with a CTA
  to post grids in the comments.
