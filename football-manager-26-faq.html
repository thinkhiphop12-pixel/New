Football Manager 26 FAQ

Platforms, Game Pass, cross-play, mobile, price and whether it's actually any good — answered plainly.

Updated 11 August 2026 · BALLKNW

Short answer

Football Manager 26 launched on 4 November 2025 for PC, Mac, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and on Nintendo Switch as FM26 Touch on 4 December. It's on Xbox Game Pass from day one, mobile is Netflix-exclusive, and Xbox–PlayStation cross-play works for online careers.

Where you can play it

FM26 is out everywhere. The confusion is that "Football Manager 26" is really four different builds with different names, and which one you get depends on the device.

The PC version is the full-depth game. Console and Touch are streamlined builds — fewer leagues loaded, a simplified interface, and less granular tactical control. If you want the version people mean when they talk about FM, that's the PC one.

Game Pass, Netflix and Apple Arcade

More people can play FM26 without buying it than in any previous year, which is worth knowing before you pay for it.

Xbox Game Pass

FM26 joined Game Pass on day one, on both PC and console. If your subscription covers the platform you play on, it's included — no separate purchase.

Netflix

Football Manager 26 Mobile is a Netflix exclusive on iOS and Android. It's bundled with any Netflix plan, with no ads and no in-app purchases. There is no standalone paid mobile version this year, so if you're searching for one on the App Store, that's why you can't find it.

Apple Arcade

FM26 Touch is on Apple Arcade, covering iPhone, iPad and Mac under one subscription.

On price: PC and console are a normal paid release, but between those three subscriptions a lot of people already have access. Check the official Football Manager site for current regional pricing before buying.

Cross-play and saves

FM26 added cross-play between Xbox and PlayStation for online careers — new for the series, and the answer most people are actually looking for. But it's partial, not universal:

  • Xbox and PlayStation players can share an online career
  • PC and console are separate builds — saves don't transfer between them
  • Touch and Mobile are separate again, with their own save files

So "is FM26 cross-platform?" is yes for Xbox–PlayStation online play, and no for moving a save from your PC to your console.

Is it any good now?

Worth being straight about this: FM26 had a bad launch. Stability problems, match freezes, Mac crashes, and an interface overhaul that a lot of long-time players disliked. Early reviews reflected that, and the "has FM26 been fixed" searches exist for a reason.

It's in much better shape now. Sports Interactive has shipped a steady run of updates — one carried over 300 fixes alone, covering match freezes, crashes on M1 and Intel Macs, contract logic, match reports and in-game editor bugs. Later patches reworked international management.

If you bounced off it in November, it isn't the same game. And if you're on Game Pass or Netflix, you can find that out without spending anything.

Quick answers

When did Football Manager 26 come out?

4 November 2025 on PC, Mac, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. FM26 Touch arrived on Nintendo Switch on 4 December 2025. It's out now on every platform.

Is Football Manager 26 on Game Pass?

Yes — day one, on both PC and console. If your Game Pass subscription covers the platform you play on, it's included at no extra cost.

Is Football Manager 26 cross-platform?

Partly. Xbox and PlayStation players can share an online career, which is new this year. Saves don't transfer between the PC game and the console versions.

Is Football Manager 26 on PS5?

Yes, released alongside PC and Xbox on 4 November 2025. The console edition is a separate, streamlined build rather than a port of the PC game.

How do I play Football Manager 26 on mobile?

FM26 Mobile is exclusive to Netflix on iOS and Android, included with any Netflix plan. FM26 Touch is on Apple Arcade. There's no standalone paid mobile version this year.

How much does Football Manager 26 cost?

Depends how you play it. PC and console are a standard paid release, but it's included with Game Pass, Netflix and Apple Arcade — so check whether you already have access before buying. Regional pricing is on the official Football Manager store.

Has Football Manager 26 been fixed?

Largely. One update alone shipped over 300 fixes covering match freezes, Mac crashes, contract logic and editor bugs, and later patches reworked international management. It's a different game from the November launch build.

Is Football Manager 26 any good?

Now, yes — at launch, no. It shipped with genuine stability problems and the reviews were fair. Several months of patches later it's in good shape. Game Pass and Netflix let you judge for yourself for free.

How do I get the Football Manager 26 beta?

You can't — FM26 is fully released. The beta was an early-access build for people who pre-ordered from participating digital retailers, and it ended at launch.
