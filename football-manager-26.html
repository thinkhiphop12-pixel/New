The short version

Football Manager 26 is Sports Interactive and SEGA's flagship club-management sim, released in November 2025. It's the biggest reset in the series' history: FM25 was cancelled outright, and FM26 arrived as the first game on a new Unity-based engine, with a rebuilt interface, a new match presentation, and — for the first time — playable women's football.

It's a paid, installed game with a serious time commitment. If that's what you're after, buy it on Steam or Epic. If what you actually want is to manage a club right now, for free, without installing anything — that's what Gaffa is for, and it runs in the tab you already have open.

What's actually new in FM26

New engine

The whole game moved to Unity after two decades on in-house tech. That's why FM25 was scrapped — the rebuild took longer than one release cycle.

Women's football

Fully playable women's leagues and clubs for the first time in the series, alongside the men's game in the same save.

Rebuilt UI and match engine

A redesigned tile-based interface and a new match presentation layer — the most visible change from FM24 for returning players.

Where it runs

PC (Steam, Epic), Xbox, FM26 Touch, and FM26 Mobile — the mobile edition is distributed through Netflix Games, so it needs a Netflix subscription rather than a separate purchase.

FM26 — depth first

Quick answers

When did Football Manager 26 come out?

November 2025, developed by Sports Interactive and published by SEGA — the first release after FM25 was cancelled, and the first on the new Unity-based engine.

Is Football Manager 26 free?

No — it's a paid game on PC and consoles, though it appears in subscription catalogues in some regions and FM26 Mobile comes with Netflix. If you want genuinely free, browser managers like Gaffa cost nothing and need no account.

What platforms is FM26 on?

PC via Steam and Epic, Xbox, FM26 Touch, and FM26 Mobile through Netflix Games on iOS and Android.

What's a good free alternative to FM26?

Gaffa (free, browser, no account), or free-to-start online managers like OFM and Top Eleven if you want persistent leagues against real people. Full comparison in our free football manager guide.
